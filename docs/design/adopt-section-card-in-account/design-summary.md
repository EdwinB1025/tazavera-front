## 2. End state per account section (refine-page-surfaces §5.3, static band)

Every section keeps its landmark: `<section aria-labelledby={headingId}>` around the card, named by its `h2` (user-account D9, unchanged).

| Part | Fill | Border | Radius | Padding | Gap / content |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Header band (`CardAside`) | `surface-section` (`cream-200` / `night-petrol-850`); **no hover change** (static band) | 1px `border-subtle` at its bottom | top corners from the card's clip; bottom square | 1rem on every side below 768px; from 768px 1rem top and bottom, 1.5rem left and right | flex column 0.5rem apart: `h2` (`type-subheading`, `text-accent`), then the description (`type-body`, `text-primary`) when the section has one |
| Body (`CardContent`) | `surface-card` | none | none | below 768px 1rem left, right and top, 1.25rem bottom; from 768px 1.5rem on every side | the section's content in a flex column 1.5rem apart (today one child per section) |

Resulting alignment (computed): the heading and the body content start at the same inset from the card's edge: 1rem + 1px border below 768px, 1.5rem + 1px from 768px.

**Page composition** (`AccountScreen` / `AccountClient`):
- `PageSection surface="page"`, column full width up to 32rem, start-aligned, flex column (unchanged).
- Heading block (`h1` + intro, 0.5rem apart, on the backdrop, no box), then **2rem**, then the sections group (unchanged distance).
- **Sections group:** a flex column of the four sections **1rem apart** (today they are direct children of the 2rem column). The backdrop shows in the gaps. No fill, border, padding or radius on the group.

**Responsive** (computed; refine-page-surfaces §5.4):
- 375px: column 343px; band and body content width 309px (343 − 2 × 1px − 2 × 16px). Headings and descriptions wrap. No horizontal scroll.
- From 768px: column 32rem (512px), start-aligned; content width 462px (512 − 2 − 48).
- 1024px and wider: unchanged (the section variant is always stacked).

**Contrast** (refine-page-surfaces §4.2, measured): `text-accent` on `surface-section` 6.11 traditional / 6.17 dark; `text-primary` on `surface-section` 5.72 / 11.97. Both pairs are registered in both matrices of `theme-tokens.test.ts` on `develop`. Body pairs on `surface-card` are unchanged. No new pair, no new token. The border, separator, backdrop and shadow are decorative (not gated).

## 3. What the user sees change

On `/{locale}/account`, authenticated, both themes:
1. Each section loses the 4px green leading edge; the card has a uniform 1px subtle border instead.
2. The heading and description sit in a cream-200 band (`night-petrol-850` in dark) across the top of the card, with a 1px separator below it; before, they sat on the card body.
3. Insets below 768px: 1rem instead of 1.5rem (band and body sides and top; the body bottom 1.25rem). From 768px the side insets stay 1.5rem; the band's top and bottom padding is 1rem.
4. Distance between the heading block and its content inside a section: band bottom padding + separator + body top padding (1rem + 1px + 1rem below 768px; 1rem + 1px + 1.5rem from 768px) instead of 1.5rem on one surface.
5. Distance between consecutive sections: 1rem instead of 2rem; the blue-green backdrop shows between them.
6. Content width inside a section: 309px instead of 291px at 375px; 462px instead of 460px from 768px.

Nothing changes in: the heading block, the loading and signed-out cards (Open 1), the register page (Open 1), any text, focus behaviour, landmark, accessible name or request.
