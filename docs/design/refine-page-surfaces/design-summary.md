## 0. Settled inputs (not re-decided here)

1. **Backdrop.** The shell's main area stops being cream (`surface-page` = `cream-100` today) and becomes a light blue-green background, a step between the header/sidebar green and the cream of forms and cards (Product owner, 2026-10-07, point 1).
2. **Header band.** Each section header leaves the cream and sits in its own band, distinct from the cream body; in the MVC each axis header is a `tz-card-section` band with square bottom corners (`evaluation-axis.blade.php:16`) (point 2).
3. **Delicate border.** The section body has a 1px `border-subtle` border (`sage-800` at 15%; the MVC `tz-card-section` uses the same pattern at 15%, `app.css:808`) (point 3).
4. **Sections hang from the backdrop.** Sections hang from the background inside a body container, separated by a gap, as in the MVC evaluation form (`evaluation-form.blade.php:1`, `flex flex-col gap-4`); the page should look less static. It applies to views in general (forms, detail, profile), not only evaluations (point 4).
5. **Catalog list:** one centered column; several columns were only for the landing page (decisions log "catálogo en una columna", 2026-10-07). Complementary decision (Product owner, 2026-10-07; `tazavera-front-catalog/docs/design/add-offering-catalog/handoff.md` Q2 "UPDATE 2026-10-07"): **no fixed width**. The column is as wide as the left and right margins leave inside the results region, and the backdrop shows in those margins. What matters is that the side margins exist and that the shell integrates the backdrop. The margins are this change's body-container spacing (§5.5). add-offering-catalog's round 1 point 4 only drops the grid and centres the single column.
6. **Page layout D14:** full-width sections, a centred container of at most 72rem, side padding 1rem / 2rem (from 768px), vertical padding 3rem / 4rem, explicit gaps between groups (decisions log D14; `content-structure` "Page sections"; `PageSection.tsx`).
7. **Shared collapsible:** full-width header button, title at the start, chevron at the end, `aria-expanded` / `aria-controls`, 0.25s animation (`content-structure` "Collapsible section"; `Collapsible.tsx`).
8. Design rules: semantic tokens only; `focus-visible:focus-ring` on every interactive control; disabled = 50% opacity; texts from the message catalogs as props; icons only through `src/shared/ui/icons.ts`.

## 1. Scope and capabilities

Change-id: **`refine-page-surfaces`** (verb + object, kebab-case). It does two things, both named by "page surfaces": it restores the backdrop and adds the section surface the views hang from. No more precise single name covers both; kept.

1. **Backdrop** — the light theme's page background becomes the blue-green backdrop (§3, Open 1–2). Capability **theming** (`globals.css`, `theme-tokens.test.ts`); the observable statement lives in **app-shell** ("Shell layout and main area": the main area shows the backdrop).
2. **Section card** — a `section` variant of the shared `Card` (`src/shared/ui/Card.tsx`): cream body, 1px `border-subtle`, header band on `surface-section`, always stacked (§5.3). Capability **content-structure** (new requirement "Section card", plus a consumer rule in "Page sections" that feature views hang their sections from the backdrop).
3. **Composition rule for views** — how a view composes the backdrop, its header block and its sections (§5.2). Stated in **content-structure**; applied by each feature in its own change (§9).

No interface text, no contract operation, no dependency.

## 2. Out of scope

- Migrating the screens of the active branches (catalog, evaluations, management, account, public evaluations): each adopts the pattern in its own round or artifact update (§9, Open 3).
- The catalog's own files (`OfferingsScreen.tsx`, the grid constant): the single column is catalog's round 1 point 4. This change supplies the side margins and the backdrop the column sits in (§5.5).
- Landing and About composition (Open 4: they keep their bands by default).
- Removing the now-redundant `surface-inverse` token, and the unused `SidebarInset` (`Sidebar.tsx:197-208`, also `bg-surface-page`): not touched.
- The focus indicator: the shared `focus-ring` keeps its `focus-offset` gap (`cream-100` in the light theme), so a control focused directly on the backdrop shows a 2px cream gap between it and the ring (visible on the back link in the mock-up). The ring itself passes against the backdrop (§4.1). No change proposed; noted so Product owner sees it in the mock-up.

Decisions in this table that the summary settles (criterion B):
- **L1 is not painted.** Point 4 says the sections hang "from the background inside a body container"; a filled container would put the sections on cream again and contradict points 1 and 4. The MVC source has no painted wrapper. Semantic reading, settled.
- **No accent edge on a section card.** The content `Card` keeps its 4px `border-accent` leading edge; the section variant replaces it with the uniform 1px `border-subtle` of point 3. The MVC `.tz-form-main` and `.tz-card-section` have uniform borders. Settled from the source.
- **Shadow:** the `Card`'s existing `shadow-lg`, the value add-offering-management §6.3 already chose for the same MVC panel (`shadow-xl` in the MVC). Settled by reuse.
- **Body fill `surface-card`, not `surface-form`.** The section body is a card surface (`cream-100`); the MVC `.tz-form-main` fill is `surface-form` mixed 2% towards black, which lands on `cream-100`'s lightness rather than `cream-50`'s. Settled by reuse of the `Card` fill; add-offering-management's local panel uses `surface-form` and adopts the variant (§9).
- **Mechanism for L0:** repoint the existing `surface-page` token in the light theme (Open 1, option a). Every pair registered against `surface-page` then re-measures against the backdrop by itself, the shell and `PageSection` keep their classes, and the `app-shell` scenario "the main area, with the page surface, reaches from the header to the bottom" stays true.

The backdrop, the section border, the band separator and the shadow are decorative: no contrast gate applies (theming spec, "Decorative separators are exempt"; criterion D). The band is identified as a toggle by its title and chevron, as add-collapsible-section D8 already settled; its fill against the card is not gated.

## 5. Layout and spacing (criterion A)

Breakpoints: below 768px (`md`), from 768px, from 1024px (`lg`), 1280px (capture). End states. The build chooses the utilities.

### 5.1 L0 Backdrop

- The shell's main area (`SessionShell.tsx:32`) and every `PageSection surface="page"` show `surface-page`. No padding, margin, border or radius of its own; `PageSection` supplies the spacing (§5.2).
- With the sidebar open (from `md`), the sidebar keeps its own surface; the content column beside it shows the backdrop.

### 5.2 L1 Body container (a view's composition)

- One `PageSection surface="page"` per view: full width of the content region; vertical padding 3rem below 768px and 4rem from it; a centred container at most 72rem wide with side padding 1rem below 768px and 2rem from it (content-structure "Page sections", unchanged).
- Inside the container, a flex column of top-level blocks, **2rem apart**: the header block, then the sections group, then the actions row when the view has one. Source: add-specialist-evaluations `EvaluationForm.tsx` `FORM_CLASSES` (`gap-8`) and add-offering-management §6.3 (header block → form panel 2rem).
- **Sections group:** a flex column of section cards, **1rem apart** (MVC `evaluation-form.blade.php:1`, `gap-4`; `EvaluationForm.tsx` `GROUPS_CLASSES`, `gap-4`). The backdrop shows in the gap.
- **Header block** (on the backdrop, no box): back link, eyebrow, heading and captions as each view settles them (for example `EvaluationForm.tsx` `HEADER_CLASSES`; add-offering-catalog §6.3). Unchanged by this change.
- **Width:** the column fills the container. A view that settled a reading width keeps it (add-offering-management: at most 40rem from `md`, left-aligned; add-user-account: at most 32rem, left-aligned). This change sets no new width.
- **No `surface="section"` band in a feature view.** Feature views hang their content from the backdrop. The section background remains for Landing and About (Open 4).

### 5.4 Responsive behaviour

- **375px:** the column is 343px wide (375 − 2 × 16, computed); cards fill it; the band's title block wraps (`flex-wrap`). The mock-up measures a document width of 375px at 375px: no horizontal scroll.
- **From 768px:** side padding 2rem; band and body padding switch as in §5.3; two-column content inside a body is the consumer's (for example `EvaluationGroupCard` descriptive and affective parts).
- **From 1024px:** no change for the section variant (always stacked).
- **1280px:** the container reaches its 72rem cap; the content column is 1088px wide (72rem − 2 × 2rem, computed), centred.

### 5.5 The catalog list on the backdrop (§0.5)

## 6. Components

### 6.1 `Card` — new `variant="section"` (content-structure)

- **Reuse first (criterion C):** the `Card` already owns the regions (aside, content, footer), the stacked layout, the aside separator and the container radius, and it is what the evaluation groups, account sections, catalog consensus cards and management lists use today. A separate "panel" component would be a second container primitive; the variant is preferred. The exact prop name is the architect's; this summary calls it `variant="section"`, default `"content"` (today's card, unchanged).
- **Variants:** `content` (default, unchanged: accent edge, `p-6` regions, default or stacked layout); `section` (§5.3).
- **States:** rest; band hover (collapsible band only); header focused (shared focus ring on the `CollapsibleHeader` button); collapsed / expanded (from `Collapsible`). No disabled state of its own.
- **Accessible names:** the card is not interactive and has none. A consumer that needs a landmark wraps it in `<section aria-labelledby>` pointing at the band's heading, as `AccountSection.tsx` does. A collapsible band's button takes its name from the title (Collapsible spec).
- **Texts:** none; every text comes from the consumer.

### 6.2 `Collapsible` (unchanged)

It composes into the section card as `EvaluationGroupCard` already does: `Collapsible` around the card, `CollapsibleHeader` inside the band, `CollapsibleBody` around `CardContent`. No change to its spec or classes.

### 6.3 `PageSection` (unchanged)

The `page` surface now shows the backdrop through the token. The `section` surface (`cream-200` full-width band) remains, for Landing and About only (§5.2, Open 4). Its tests (`bg-surface-page`, `bg-surface-section`) stay true.

## 7. Texts

None. The change adds no interface text; every heading, description and label comes from the consumer's message catalog (es, en, ca) as props.
